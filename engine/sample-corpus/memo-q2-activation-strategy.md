## TL;DR

Trial-to-paid conversion fell from 28% to 19% across Q4 → Q1 in the SMB
and mid-market cohorts. Q2 priority is to recover the gap, not to push
new top-of-funnel acquisition.

## What the data says

- Trial signups are flat. Conversion is the leak.
- 41% of trial workspaces never reach 3 active seats.
- The strongest predictor of conversion is *number of distinct projects
  with activity in week 1*. Workspaces that hit 2+ projects with real
  activity by day 7 convert at >50%. Workspaces with 0–1 convert at <10%.
- Trials where the admin uses our Jira or Asana import convert at 38%
  vs. 14% for trials that don't import.

## Hypotheses (not validated)

1. Admins are blocked early on plan/seat decisions and never invite the
   team, so the trial never gets used.
2. Data migration friction kills momentum in the first 72 hours, before
   the value is felt.
3. Without an anchor "first artifact" (a board, a sprint, a roadmap),
   even imported workspaces don't reach activation thresholds.

## Bets we're considering

- Onboarding v2 (PM is scoping; see PRD intent doc)
- Lift the Jira-API import out of beta
- A done-for-you onboarding service for accounts with >25 seats

## What this memo deliberately does not do

It does not commit to a specific solution. It frames the gap and lists
candidate bets. Picking is done in the operating review next Tuesday.
