## Context

RocketCo signed up, churned after 11 days. Migrated from Jira. Jordan was
both the buyer and the team's sole PM.

## Transcript (abridged)

**PM:** Why did you sign up?

**Jordan:** Jira was insane for a six-person team. I read about Pebble on
a list. The marketing said "set up in five minutes" — I figured I'd just
try it on a Sunday afternoon.

**PM:** What happened on that Sunday?

**Jordan:** I got through signup fine. Then I needed to bring my issues
over. The Jira CSV export is a mess — fields don't line up. I spent
maybe an hour fighting with the import and then gave up. I'll come back
next weekend, I told myself. I didn't.

**PM:** What stopped you from coming back?

**Jordan:** Honestly the import was a blocker. If I can't get my work
into the tool, the tool doesn't exist for me. I'm not going to manually
recreate 80 tickets.

**PM:** Did you talk to anyone on the team about it?

**Jordan:** No. It was my call, I was going to surprise them with the
switch. So it died on my desk.

**PM:** Did the trial sandbox or sample data help?

**Jordan:** Not really. The sample project is cute but it's not my work.
I can't tell if a tool fits my actual situation by clicking around a
demo workspace.

**PM:** If we had had a native Jira import that preserved the project
structure, would you have stuck with it?

**Jordan:** Probably yes, for the trial. Whether we'd have bought is a
different question — that depends on how the team felt about it. But I
wouldn't have churned in the trial.

**PM:** Anything else from the trial that bugged you?

**Jordan:** The roadmap view. I needed something like Jira's advanced
roadmap and I couldn't find an equivalent. Might exist, I didn't look
hard. The import killed me first.
