## Context

GreenCo signed up six weeks ago and rolled out to ~40 people. Sam ran the
rollout. Migrated from Asana. They're our largest paying account from this
cohort.

## Transcript (abridged)

**PM:** Walk me through how the rollout went.

**Sam:** It went fast. I spent maybe a day setting up the workspace
structure, then we did a Monday all-hands where I demoed it for fifteen
minutes, then everyone was in by Tuesday.

**PM:** What made it fast for you?

**Sam:** Two things. One — I've done this rollout three times at three
different companies, so I know what tabs to click. I'm not the user you
should optimize for. Two — your SSO with Okta worked first try. That's
the single biggest piece of "is this going to be ops-painful or not."

**PM:** What about importing prior work?

**Sam:** We didn't. We decided to start fresh and let Asana die on its
own. That's controversial and most teams won't do it, but for us the
prior data was mostly stale.

**PM:** Were there moments where you got stuck?

**Sam:** The permissions model. We have contractors. I wanted them in
specific projects only, not the whole workspace, and that flow is buried
two settings menus deep. I figured it out, but I had to figure it out.

**PM:** Anything that surprised you about how the team adopted it?

**Sam:** A lot of people didn't use Pebble for the first two weeks. They
kept working in Slack and email. Then someone built a really nice bug
board in Pebble and word spread and now everyone's in there. The product
adopted itself the moment one person showed what good looked like in it.

**PM:** Anything you wish had been different in the setup?

**Sam:** A native Okta-provisioning flow would have saved me an hour.
Other than that I'm a happy admin.
