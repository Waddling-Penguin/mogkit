## Context

Acme signed up three weeks ago. 14 seats. Migrated from Trello. Maya is the
team lead and was the buyer/champion.

## Transcript (abridged)

**PM:** Walk me through the first day. What did you actually do when you
signed up?

**Maya:** Honestly? I signed up on a Thursday, then I didn't open Pebble
again for four days. I was waiting until I could sit down and do it
properly. I think that's pretty normal — nobody wants to half-set-up a
new tool in the middle of a sprint.

**PM:** What were you waiting to do?

**Maya:** Get the team in. I didn't want to start a workspace and then
have to redo it once everyone was in. So I needed admin stuff first — who
can invite, what permissions, that kind of thing. And I couldn't see that
in the trial — it just dropped me into a sample project.

**PM:** When you did come back, what happened?

**Maya:** I tried to invite the team and it asked me to upgrade. Which —
fine, that's the model — but I didn't know it was going to do that before
I picked the plan. And we had budget approval for one tier and the
invite limit on it was lower than our headcount. So I had to go back to
finance, which is another two days.

**PM:** Anything else that slowed you down?

**Maya:** The import. We had like 200 cards in Trello. The CSV import
worked but lost all the comment history. We ended up keeping Trello open
for a month as the read-only archive.

**PM:** What would have made the first week easier?

**Maya:** Honestly, just a sandbox that showed me what admin would look
like. Or even a doc that said "here's how invites and seats work, decide
this before you start." I don't need it to be in the product. I just
need to know it exists before I burn a week.

**PM:** And what's the team using it for now?

**Maya:** Sprint planning and the bug triage board. We didn't move
roadmap over yet — that's still in Notion. Not sure we will.
