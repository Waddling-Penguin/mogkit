## Where to start

Pick the question you are facing. Each skill takes what you paste in and
returns the gaps. None of them writes the finished plan, page or price.

**Before you build**

- **`icp-sharpen`** tests who you say the product is for against the
  accounts you have won, lost and kept. It names who to turn away.
- **`positioning-check`** tests a positioning draft against April
  Dunford's five components and marks each one as evidence or opinion.

**Before you price**

- **`pricing-pressure-test`** checks that what you charge for matches the
  value a customer gets, that a customer can predict the bill, and what
  your heaviest users do to the margin. It pays particular attention to
  AI features that cost money on every use.

**Before you launch**

- **`launch-tier`** sizes the launch. A fix gets a changelog line. A
  pricing change gets every team briefed. Most mistakes are picking the
  wrong size.
- **`launch-readiness`** then finds the gaps in the plan: what nobody
  owns, what cannot be rolled back, who has not been told, and the
  thresholds for pulling the launch.

**After you launch**

- **`win-loss-review`** reads deal notes and call transcripts and reports
  why deals went the way they did, with a quote behind every reason.
- **`agent-readiness`** checks whether an AI assistant can find,
  understand, recommend and use your product, and where it gets facts
  wrong.
